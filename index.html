Quick answers

Good to know.

What can I promote?

Examples include flyers, local events, products, services, grand openings, community activities, and special offers. Promotions must be lawful and accurately described.

Can I do this in my spare time?

That’s the idea. Some tasks may be done remotely, such as sharing approved content online; others may involve local flyer distribution or event promotion. Each task has its own requirements.

How do I get paid?

Payment depends on the specific task and the agreement with the business. Confirm the amount, requirements, and payment timing before starting. ReachCrew does not guarantee that tasks or earnings will be available.

Does signing up guarantee paid work?

No. Submitting your information is an introduction, not a guarantee of selection, tasks, or income. Never pay an upfront fee to access a supposed job.
